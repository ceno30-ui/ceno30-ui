A personal Obsidian vault built to keep school work, academic notes, and life interests organized in one clean, easy-to-navigate place.

## ✨ Overview

This vault is structured around two main areas:

- School organization and class notes
- Personal life, hobbies, and interests

It’s designed to be imported directly into an Obsidian workspace and used as a manageable digital home for everyday learning and personal tracking.

## 📚 School focus

The vault currently includes these classes:

- Biology
- Freshman Seminar
- English
- Math
- History
- Spanish
- Tech

## 🧠 Personal areas

- About Me
- Sports
- Bikes
- Personal Dashboard

## 🗂️ Vault structure

```text
Conor_Obsidian_Vault/
├── README.md
├── Personal/
│   ├── Personal Dashboard.md
│   ├── Bikes/
│   │   └── Bikes.md
│   ├── Profile/
│   │   └── About Me.md
│   └── Sports/
│       └── Sports.md
└── School/
    ├── School Dashboard.md
    ├── Biology/
    ├── English/
    ├── Freshman Seminar/
    ├── History/
    ├── Math/
    ├── Spanish/
    └── Tech/
```

## 🚀 Why this project works

- Organized by subject and category
- Easy to expand with new notes and pages
- Keeps school info separate from personal life
- Built for a clean, import-ready Obsidian workflow
- Designed to stay useful as a growing long-term vault

## 📌 Notes

This repository reflects the information provided for the vault project. It is meant to be a clean, usable structure rather than a fabricated or exaggerated personal profile.
