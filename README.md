## About Me

I’m a student who likes staying active, trying new things, and spending time outdoors. One of my favorite things to do is snowboard. There’s something about being out on the mountain, feeling the cold air, and riding through fresh snow that makes everything else feel more exciting. Snowboarding is a big part of how I recharge and push myself to keep improving.

I like the mix of athleticism, creativity, and focus that comes with it. Whether I’m learning a new line, cruising down a run, or just being out in the mountains with friends, it’s one of the best ways for me to enjoy life outside of school.

Outside of snowboarding, I also love staying active in other ways. I’ve grown up playing baseball, I’ve been in soccer for a long time, and I also enjoy biking around with friends. I like having a balance of school, sports, outdoor time, and just being able to have fun and keep moving.
